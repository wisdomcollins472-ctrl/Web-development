About Kayzplace

A neighbourhood shop with serious craft

Kayzplace started with one chair, one clipper and a simple promise: nobody leaves this shop with a cut they don't love. Ten years later, we're still on Bode Thomas Street — same hands, sharper standards.

  • Every fade mapped to your head shape — not a template.
  • Sterilised tools, fresh blades, single-use collars.
  • Beard work by barbers who actually wear beards.
  • Cold drinks, charger points and real conversation.
