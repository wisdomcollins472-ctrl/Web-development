At 42 Oba Akran Avenue, Chicken Republic serves quick, satisfying meals centred on our signature fried chicken — crispy on the outside, juicy inside and seasoned for the Lagos palate. It’s the same Republic taste you know, with friendly counter service for dine-in and takeaway.
